How to use macro:
1. In UGS, click windows, then macros.
2. A macro window should show up in the lower left window of UGS.
3. Select the macros window.
4. Right click on the space in the macros window.
5. Select create macros/edit macros.
6. In the center of the macro window that popped up, click the +Add button on the lower right.
7. Paste the string below on the GCode column.
    38.2 Z-10 F50; G92 Z0.1; G0 Z10
8. Click apply and exit the window.
9. A macro button containing the gcode you created will be present in the macros window.
10. Everytime you switch drill bits, just click the macro button and it will automatically calibrate itself.
